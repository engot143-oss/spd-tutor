Cleaning comes first.

Explore why cleaning matters before disinfection or sterilization. These are learning concepts, not device instructions.

A 30-second refresher

Cleaning removes soil. Remaining soil can interfere with later treatment. For real work, follow the device and equipment manufacturers’ instructions for use (IFUs), your training, and facility policy.
